# Store of Love

**A bara romance visual novel set in a 24-hour convenience store.**

Late nights. Neon lights. Rain on the pavement.  
And the quiet moments between customers that change everything.

---

## About the Game

**Store of Love** is a narrative-driven visual novel focused on male romance (bara).  
You work the night shift at a small convenience store called *Night & Day*. Between stocking shelves, dealing with regulars and the occasional chaos of 3 a.m., you form unexpected connections with the people who walk through those automatic doors.

The story blends everyday tenderness with deeper emotional bonds, quiet longing, and the kind of intimacy that only appears when the city is asleep.

### Core Features (Planned)

- **Branching romance routes** with multiple endings
- **Affinity / Relationship system** – your choices shape how each character sees you
- **CG Gallery** – unlockable illustrations
- **Dynamic music** that changes according to character and mood
- **Full bilingual support** (Spanish & English)
- Custom Dialogic style adapted to the neon + rainy night aesthetic

---

## Setting & Atmosphere

Inspired by late-night convenience stores, wet streets reflecting neon signs, and the soft loneliness of the night shift.  
The visual direction aims for a warm, slightly melancholic, intimate feeling — watercolor/illustration style mixed with soft neon glow.
